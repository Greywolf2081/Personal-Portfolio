A Little About Me

My name is Tyler Kurtz, and I'm currently a student in the Computer Information Technologies (CIT) Program at Lethbridge Polytechnic.

A recent passion of mine has become bass guitar, I started in January of 2025 and have enjoyed it since. In March of 2026 my brother and I started a band called 'Hot Wet Mud' where we have found a drummer and a secondary guitarist. We have played at major venues like Dragon Boats Festival, Street Wheelers, Honkers Fest, and are looking for more. We also host jams all over Lethbridge.

I've always had an interest in computers and technology, and I've decided to pursue that interest by developing my knowledge and skills in the IT field. Through my studies, I'm growing my knowledge and experience with programming, website design, databases, networking, and server management.

I'm someone who enjoys learning how things work, solving problems, and taking on challenges that allow me to develop new skills. I particularly enjoy working on projects where I can take an idea and turn it into something functional.

My Background

Before pursuing my education into technology, I spent several years working in accounting. I started at my previous workplace while still in high school, initially working as an office cleaner.

Over time, I took on additional responsibilities, including bookkeeping and answering phones. Eventually, I worked my way into an accounting position where I later became the manager of the Accounting Department, in doing so I was responsible for managing accounts, working with clients, handling contracts, and helping bring new employees onto the team.

That experience has taught me a lot about responsibility, organization, communication, and solving problems that come up. Although my career path has changed, those skills are still valuable and something I bring into my education and future career and something I will use for the rest of my life.

My Interests

Outside of school and work, I enjoy spending time outdoors, especially in the mountains. I also enjoy spending some of my free time gaming with friends.

Gaming has been a big influence in my interest in technology, particularly game development. I've become interested in what goes into creating games, from programming and designing characters to building environments and developing gameplay systems.

I enjoy exploring new technologies and experimenting with different projects, especially when they give me an opportunity to learn something new.

My Future Goals

My current goal is to build a career in the technology industry and continue developing the skills I've gained through the CIT program.

One of my long term ambitions is to work as a game programmer or designer at a AAA video game studio. I'd love to be part of a team that creates games which are experienced and enjoyed by people around the world.

I'm still exploring the different areas of programming and game development to determine where I want to specialize. For now, I'm focused on learning as much as possible, gaining practical experience, and building a strong foundation for my future.
